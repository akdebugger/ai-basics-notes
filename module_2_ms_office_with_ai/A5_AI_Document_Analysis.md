# MODULE 2 — PART A: MS WORD WITH AI
## Topic A5: AI Document Analysis (Summarization, Extraction, Comparison Aur Document Review)

---

### 1. Concept (Detailed Explanation in Hinglish)

MS Word mein lambi reports, vendor agreements, ya compliance policies kholte hi 40-50 pages ka text dekh kar aksar sir ghoomne lagta hai. AI Document Analysis ka matlab hai AI ko ek digital legal aur business analyst ki tarah use karna jo lambe Word documents ko scan karke seconds mein summarize karta hai, critical data points extract karta hai, do documents ke beech ke differences highlight karta hai, aur formal review commentary taiyar karta hai.

Chaliye iske 5 core capabilities ko detail mein samajhte hain:

#### 1. Document Summarization
- **Kya hai:** Kisi 10-page ya 50-page ke document ke main objectives, core findings aur final conclusions ko 1-page Executive Summary ya 5 bullet points mein condense karna.
- **Kyun zaroori hai:** Busy directors aur senior managers ke paas poori file padhne ka waqt nahi hota; unhe decision lene ke liye "TL;DR" summary chahiye hoti hai.
- **AI se kaise karein:** Document text paste karein ya file attach karein aur prompt dein: *"Provide a 150-word Executive Summary focusing strictly on strategic deliverables and financial liabilities."*

#### 2. Document Analysis (Deep Diagnostic)
- **Kya hai:** Document ke underlying arguments, tone, risk factors, loopholes, aur logical consistency ko evaluate karna.
- **Kyun zaroori hai:** Kisi vendor contract mein aamtaur par clauses bohot ghuma-fira kar likhe hote hain. Analysis se pata chalta hai ki kya koi clause hamari company ke liye risky hai (jaise: unlimited indemnity ya one-sided termination).
- **AI se kaise karein:** Prompt dein: *"Analyze this agreement from a buyer's risk perspective. Identify top 3 operational and legal risk clauses."*

#### 3. Information Extraction
- **Kya hai:** Text ke samandar mein se specific facts, figures, dates, penalty amounts, contact emails, ya statutory clauses ko pinpoint karke nikaalna.
- **Kyun zaroori hai:** Manual reading mein dates ya payment thresholds miss hone ka bohot risk rehta hai. Extraction data ko structured tables mein arrange kar deta hai.
- **AI se kaise karein:** Prompt dein: *"Extract all monetary penalties, payment terms, and critical deadlines into a clean 3-column table."*

#### 4. Document Comparison (Version vs Version)
- **Kya hai:** Do alag-alag versions (jaise: Version 1.0 vs Version 2.0 of a Policy, ya Supplier Proposal A vs Supplier Proposal B) ko aamne-saamne rakh kar differences track karna.
- **Kyun zaroori hai:** Jab samne wali party contract ka naya version bhejti hai, toh manually ek-ek line match karna impossible hota hai. AI batata hai: "Clause 4 mein penalty 5% se badha kar 10% kar di gayi hai."
- **AI se kaise karein:** Dono versions provide karein aur kahein: *"Perform a side-by-side comparative variance analysis between Version 1 and Version 2."*

#### 5. AI-Assisted Document Review
- **Kya hai:** Final submission ya board sign-off se pehle document ka comprehensive evaluation karna — kya koi critical section missing hai? Kya tone standard corporate guideline se match karti hai?
- **Kyun zaroori hai:** Zero-defect compliance aur board-level presentation readiness ensure karne ke liye.
- **AI se kaise karein:** Prompt dein: *"Act as an Executive Auditor. Review this draft policy for completeness, clarity, and compliance gaps."*

### 3. Step-by-Step Workflow in MS Word

```
┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐
│     STEP 1      │     │     STEP 2      │     │     STEP 3      │     │     STEP 4      │
│  Select / Paste │ ──> │ Define Analytic │ ──> │ Generate Matrix │ ──> │ Export to Word  │
│ Open document   │     │ Goal (Summarize,│     │ Review tables,  │     │ Paste in Word   │
│ in MS Word      │     │ Extract, Compare│     │ numbers & gaps  │     │ with commentary │
└─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘
```

1. **Step 1:** Word document kholiye aur analysis karne wale section ko copy karein (ya MS 365 Copilot mein direct file reference karein).
2. **Step 2:** AI ko clear task dein — e.g. "Extract all financial limits into a table" ya "Compare Version 1 with Version 2".
3. **Step 3:** AI dwara generate kiye gaye tabular output aur differences ko verify karein.
4. **Step 4:** Result ko MS Word document ke Executive Summary section mein clean table aur bold highlights ke sath integrate karein.

### 4. Practical Example (Version 1 vs Version 2 Comparison)

**Scenario:** Management ko dono policy versions ka quick comparative variance note submit karna hai.

**Prompt (English):**
```text
You are a Senior Corporate Policy Analyst.
Perform a comparative variance analysis between Policy Version 1.0 (2025) and Policy Version 2.0 (2026).
Generate a structured 4-column Markdown table:
Parameter | Version 1.0 (2025) | Version 2.0 (2026) | Strategic Impact of Change
Summarize the management rationale in 2 concise bullets below the table.
```

**AI Response (Sample):**
```text
| Parameter | Version 1.0 (2025) | Version 2.0 (2026) | Strategic Impact of Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eligibility Scope** | Full-time employees only | Full-time & contractual staff | More inclusive; covers project consultants. |
| **Booking Lead Time** | 7 days in advance | 14 days in advance | Maximizes early-bird airline fare discounts. |
| **Tier-1 Hotel Cap** | ₹4,000 / night | ₹5,500 / night | Realistic market inflation adjustment. |
| **Daily Food Per Diem** | ₹1,000 / day | ₹1,500 / day (Digital only) | Better meal allowance; eliminates paper handling. |
| **Intra-City Travel** | ₹1,200 / day | ₹1,800 / day (EV preference) | Promotes ESG/Green mobility goals. |
| **Submission Deadline**| 15 calendar days | 7 calendar days | Faster monthly accounting closure. |
| **Late Penalty** | Written CFO sanction | Automatic 10% deduction | Strong financial deterrent against delayed claims. |
| **Settlement Speed** | 10 business days | 5 business days | Faster reimbursement boosts employee morale. |

Management Rationale Summary:
• Inflation & Employee Welfare: Hotel and meal caps were raised by ~37-50% to reflect actual corporate living costs in metro hubs.
• Operational Tightening: Advance booking doubled to 14 days and claim submission cut to 7 days to eliminate delayed quarterly reporting.
```

### 5. Key Points (Yaad Rakhne Wali Baatein)
- **Always Use Tabular Formats for Extraction:** Data table mein dekhna 10x asan hota hai banisbat lambe paragraphs ke.
- **Identify Financial Liabilities First:** Contracts review karte waqt payment terms aur penalties sabse pehle extract karein.
- **Never Assume Missing Clauses:** Agar document mein termination clause nahi hai, toh AI ko clearly batana chahiye: *"Clause not found in text."*
- **Audit Both Sides in Comparison:** Change sirf kya badla woh nahi, balki us badlaav se company par kya financial/legal asar padega woh bhi analyze karein.

---

### 6. Common Mistakes / Dhyan Dene Wali Baatein
- **AI ke extraction par blind trust karna:** Badi numbers aur dates ko original Word text se ek baar cross-check zaroor karein.
- **Confidential M&A documents public AI par paste karna:** Agar document top-secret corporate acquisition ka hai, toh public AI mein paste na karein (Module 1 Safety Rule!).
- **Vague comparison command dena:** Sirf *"Compare both"* bolne se superficial answer aayega; parameters specify karein (Cost, Timeline, Liability).

### 8. 🧪 PRACTICE SET — TOPIC A5

#### A. Practice Prompts (English Only)

##### Prompt 1 (Executive Summarization of Long Document):
```text
You are an Executive Business Chief of Staff.
Review the provided "Travel & Expense Policy Version 2.0" text. 
Produce an authoritative, board-level Executive Summary under 120 words.
Structure:
- Core Objective (1 sentence)
- Key Operational Upgrades (3 bullet points covering booking, hotel caps, and EV travel)
- Mandatory Compliance Directive (1 sentence regarding the 7-day submission deadline).
Tone: Authoritative, concise, executive.
```
- **Expected Output:** Board-ready 120-word executive briefing with precise bullet anchors.
- **Skill Practiced:** Document condensation and executive synthesis.
- **Follow-up Prompt:**
```text
Format this summary into a 1-page visual announcement memo suitable for displaying on corporate office bulletin boards.
```

##### Prompt 2 (Targeted Parameter Information Extraction):
```text
You are a Financial Audit Specialist.
Carefully review the text of "Travel & Expense Policy Version 2.0". 
Extract all financial limits, quantitative thresholds, and operational deadlines into a clean 3-column table:
Column 1: Operational Category (e.g., Hotel, Food, Booking Timeline)
Column 2: Prescribed Quantitative Limit / Threshold
Column 3: Statutory / Accounting Requirement (e.g., GST e-invoice, CFO approval)
Strictly extract only facts mentioned in the text; do not invent or extrapolate numbers.
```
- **Expected Output:** Comprehensive quantitative parameter matrix extracted directly from the policy.
- **Skill Practiced:** Structured entity and numeric extraction.
- **Follow-up Prompt:**
```text
Identify any ambiguity in the policy where a specific procedure is not clearly stated (e.g., weekend travel rules).
```

##### Prompt 3 (Side-by-Side Comparative Variance Matrix):
```text
You are a Corporate Governance and Policy Consultant.
Conduct a detailed comparison between "Policy Version 1.0 (2025)" and "Policy Version 2.0 (2026)". 
Generate a comprehensive comparative matrix in Markdown Table format containing:
- Specific Policy Parameter
- Version 1.0 Rule
- Version 2.0 Rule
- Percentage or Absolute Increase/Decrease
- Administrative Intent Behind the Modification
Highlight in bold any change that introduces a new financial penalty or financial benefit.
```
- **Expected Output:** Deep analytical variance matrix tracking changes with strategic administrative intent.
- **Skill Practiced:** Document comparison and corporate policy auditing.
- **Follow-up Prompt:**
```text
Draft a brief 3-sentence advisory note for company employees explaining the critical changes they must follow immediately.
```

##### Prompt 4 (Contractual Risk & Loophole Analysis):
```text
You are a Commercial Contract Legal Reviewer.
Analyze the following commercial vendor agreement excerpt from a buyer's risk perspective. 
Identify 3 major contractual risks, loopholes, or one-sided liability clauses that could harm our company. 
For each risk, explain the potential business impact and propose an edited, balanced alternative clause.

[Sample Input]:
"The Supplier reserves the unilateral right to revise software subscription fees by up to 25% upon thirty days notice without requiring buyer consent. The Buyer agrees to indemnify the Supplier against all third-party claims, legal fees, and operational losses arising from software usage without any financial limitation. In the event of contract termination, all fees paid for the remaining annual period shall be deemed strictly non-refundable."
```
- **Expected Output:** High-grade legal risk audit identifying unilateral fee escalation, unlimited indemnity, and non-refundable lock-ins.
- **Skill Practiced:** Diagnostic legal analysis and risk mitigation drafting.
- **Follow-up Prompt:**
```text
Draft a formal negotiation letter to the supplier's legal counsel requesting mutual indemnity caps and a fee increase ceiling tied to retail inflation.
```

##### Prompt 5 (AI-Assisted Policy Review & Gap Identification):
```text
You are an HR and Internal Controls Specialist.
Perform a critical compliance review of "Travel & Expense Policy Version 2.0". 
Identify 3 critical corporate operational scenarios that this policy currently FAILS to address 
(such as emergency medical expenses during travel, cancellation of flights due to personal emergencies, 
or foreign currency conversion for international business trips). 
Recommend exact phrasing for 3 new sub-clauses to address these gaps.
```
- **Expected Output:** Expert gap analysis proposing actionable, audit-ready clauses for omitted workplace scenarios.
- **Skill Practiced:** Policy gap detection and comprehensive document engineering.
- **Follow-up Prompt:**
```text
Integrate these 3 newly drafted clauses into the main policy document maintaining consistent numbering and tone.
```

#### C. Improve This Prompt (Exercise & Solutions)

##### Weak Prompt 1:
`Summarize this document.`
- **Problem:** Kitna lamba summary? Kiske liye? Focus areas kya hain?
- **Model Answer:**
```text
You are an Executive Assistant to the CFO. Provide a 100-word Executive TL;DR summary of the attached vendor service contract. 
Focus strictly on payment terms, annual price escalation, and penalty clauses. Use 3 clear bullet points followed by a 1-sentence bottom-line takeaway.
[Insert contract text]
```

##### Weak Prompt 2:
`Compare these two files.`
- **Problem:** Dono files mein kya match karna hai? Format kya chahiye?
- **Model Answer:**
```text
You are a Senior Corporate Policy Auditor. Compare the following two versions of our Employee Leave Policy. 
Generate a 4-column Markdown table: Clause Name | Old Policy (2024) | New Policy (2026) | Significance of Change. 
Highlight any reduction in employee leave benefits in bold.
```

#### D. Mini Quiz (With Answers & Explanations)

1. **Document Comparison karte waqt sabse bada corporate benefit kya hota hai?**
   - A) Computer ki storage badhana
   - B) Do versions ke beech ke subtle differences, price escalations aur legal changes ko bina ghanton manual reading kiye detect karna
   - C) File ka font change karna
   - D) Sare tables delete kar dena
   *(Answer: **B** | Explanation: Automated comparison human error aur contract manipulation se protect karti hai).*

2. **Information Extraction prompt mein "Strictly extract only facts mentioned in the text" likhna kyun zaroori hai?**
   - A) AI ki typing speed badhane ke liye
   - B) AI ko hallucinate karne ya missing data assume karke fake numbers banane se rokne ke liye
   - C) English grammar test karne ke liye
   - D) Document ko print karne ke liye
   *(Answer: **B** | Explanation: Negative constraints factual fidelity ensure karte hain).*

3. **CFO ke liye document summarize karte waqt primary focus kis par hona chahiye?**
   - A) Background ki kahaniyon par
   - B) Financial liabilities, quantitative numbers, payment terms aur operational deadlines par
   - C) Office furniture par
   - D) Weather conditions par
   *(Answer: **B** | Explanation: Finance leadership cash-flow impact aur compliance deadlines par focus karti hai).*

4. **Agar kisi vendor contract mein "unlimited indemnity" clause ho, toh iska kya matlab hota hai?**
   - A) Company ko free product milega
   - B) Buyer par kisi bhi nuksan ya lawsuit par bina kisi maximum boundary limit ke karodon ka liability aa sakta hai, jo bohot risky hai
   - C) Contract 100 saal chalega
   - D) Vendor har saal bonus dega
   *(Answer: **B** | Explanation: Unlimited indemnity ek severe risk hai jise corporate lawyers cap karwate hain).*

5. **Document analysis report mein table format use karna paragraphs se behtar kyun maana jata hai?**
   - A) Kyunki table mein colors acche lagte hain
   - B) Kyunki structured grid parameters, limits aur variances ko executive reader ke liye 1 second mein scan-able banata hai
   - C) Kyunki table mein spelling mistake nahi hoti
   - D) Kyunki Word mein table mandatory hota hai
   *(Answer: **B** | Explanation: Tables data readability aur decision-making speed ko 10x improve karte hain).*

**Short Questions:**
- *Q1: Policy Version 1 aur Version 2 ke comparison mein "Administrative Intent" column ka kya mahatva hai?*  
  **Ans:** Sirf yeh janna kaafi nahi ki rule badla; intent column explain karta hai ki rule company ke fayde ke liye (cost saving, faster closing, ya employee welfare) kyun badla gaya.
- *Q2: Document mein missing sections identify karna (Gap Analysis) corporate risk management mein kaise madad karta hai?*  
  **Ans:** Jo baatein policy mein nahi likhi hoti hain (jaise emergency medical expense ya cancellation rules), unpar baad mein disputes hote hain. Gap analysis un loopholes ko pehle hi seal kar deti hai.
